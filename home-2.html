Common Risks & Damage

  • Acid fumes & terminal sulfation causing corroded home battery cables.
  • Harmonic waveform distortion causing humming noises in ceiling fans and TV flicker.
  • Dry electrolyte lead plates leading to sudden unexpected dead battery in mid-summer.
  • Generator stale fuel gumming up carburetor jets, preventing start during power failure.

Zero Fine Print

Clear Coverage Breakdown

We believe in 100% transparent terms with zero hidden charges when a blackout hits your home.

What Is Fully Covered in Every AMC
  • All doorstep service callout charges and emergency breakdown labor fees.
  • Routine electrolyte replenishment using demineralized laboratory distilled water.
  • Terminal cleaning, anti-corrosion petroleum gel coating, and bolt re-torquing.
  • Generator spark plug cleaning, carburetor pilot jet flush, and air filter dusting.
  • Inverter output voltage calibration ensuring pure 230V sine wave for home electronics.
  • Standby loaner inverter unit provided during major workshop motherboard overhauls.
Fair Exclusions (Not Covered)
  • Physical breakage or casing cracks caused by accidental external falls or moving.
  • Flooding or water immersion of equipment placed directly on low outdoor ground.
  • Tampering or attempted circuit modifications by unverified third-party handymen.
  • Replacement of fully aged battery packs exceeding their 5-year chemical lifespan.
